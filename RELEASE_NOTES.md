Skip Button 1.2.0 for Horses of Hoofprint Bay.

This mod was called Skip Training until now. The new name is the only part of the rename you will see: the files keep their names, so your settings and competition results carry over.

New in this version:

- A skipped lesson can pay the best tip, the one a client gives for an excellent lesson. In the Mods menu, Tip for a skipped lesson offers No tip, Good lesson tip and Best tip. Best tip is the new default. If you had tips switched on before, you keep the good lesson tip.
- New setting Competition skip needs. With "Won this one before", a horse can also skip a competition it has won before, even when it didn't win its last competition. It still needs a recorded best run in that competition. "Won previous competition" works as before and is the default.
- The settings page folds into two sections, "Skip rides, lessons, competitions" and "Lesson horse needs".

Requires BepInEx 6 bleeding edge build 793 (IL2CPP, Windows x64) and Mod Manager 1.3.0 or newer. To update, extract the zip into the game folder and replace the old `SkipTraining.dll`, and update Mod Manager as well.
